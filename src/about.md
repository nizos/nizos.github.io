I'm Nizar Selander, a software architect and consultant at [factor10](https://www.factor10.com),
focused on building quality, maintainable solutions with teams who care about their craft.

I'm passionate about Test-Driven Development, agentic coding, and automation, particularly where AI
innovation meets practical software engineering.

My work:

- Built
  [several of Sweden's top-ranked websites](/striving-for-performance-and-design-harmony-in-web-development/)
  in performance, security, and accessibility
- Created [Probity](https://github.com/nizos/probity) and its predecessor
  [TDD Guard](https://github.com/nizos/tdd-guard), open-source tools that hold coding agents to TDD
  and other guardrails, with 400,000+ downloads and contributions from 15 developers
- [Interviewed by Emily Bache](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bDLdZIAjH5Y) on the Modern Software
  Engineering channel about TDD with coding agents
- Given talks on agentic coding, including
  ["5 Pro Tips for Coding with Agents"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WWspNDD7nnQ)

When I'm not programming, you'll find me watching Formula 1, catching up on movies, or
binge-watching TV series.
